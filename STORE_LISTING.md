# Connect IQ Store backup instructions

Back up your Rep Counter workouts at https://backup.execureach.co/.

On your watch, open Settings > Backup / Export. Open the website on your phone and enter the code shown on the watch. Keep Garmin Connect running on your paired phone and leave the watch app open during the transfer.

Download JSON to keep a restorable backup, or CSV to view your workouts in a spreadsheet. To restore, upload a JSON backup on the website, send it to the watch, then open MENU on the watch and confirm Replace history. Restoring replaces the current history.

Transfer codes expire after 15 minutes. Keep your downloaded JSON file somewhere safe.
